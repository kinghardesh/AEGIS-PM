# User Notifications

## Background

Our users often miss important things happening in their accounts. We keep hearing that people "didn't know" something happened until it was too late. We need to close that gap and keep users informed.

## What We Want

We want to notify users appropriately about important events. When something important happens, the user should hear about it. This will improve engagement and reduce the number of "I didn't know" support tickets we get.

## Requirements

Notifications should go out for important events. They should be timely and not overwhelming — we don't want to spam people, but we also don't want them to miss anything that matters. Strike the right balance so notifications feel helpful rather than annoying.

The notifications should reach users wherever makes the most sense for them. Users should be able to stay on top of what's happening without having to constantly check the app.

## Considerations

Frequency matters. Too many notifications and people tune out or turn them off entirely. Too few and they miss things. Find the sweet spot.

Let's make sure the right people get notified about the right things at the right time.

## Outcome

Fewer missed events, fewer support tickets, more engaged users who feel in control and well-informed about what's happening in their accounts.
