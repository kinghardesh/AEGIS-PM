# Add Payments

## Overview

Add payments so users can pay for subscriptions.

## Goal

We want users to be able to pay for their subscription plans from within the
app. Right now everything is free and we need to start charging.

## Requirements

- Users should be able to pay for a subscription.
- Payment should be tied to their account.
- Once paid, the subscription should become active.

## Open Questions

- Which payment provider do we use?
- What currency/currencies do we support?
- What are the plan tiers and their prices?
- How do we handle tax?
- What is the refund policy?

## Notes

Details to be finalized with finance and legal before build.
