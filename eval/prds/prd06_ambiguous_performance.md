# Performance Improvements

## Overview

Users have been complaining that the app feels sluggish. Leadership wants us to make the app faster and improve overall performance this quarter. This is a priority initiative and we need everyone focused on speed.

## Goals

The core goal is simple: the product should feel fast. When people use it, things should just be snappy and responsive. Right now it isn't, and that's hurting our reputation. Several customers mentioned in their renewal calls that the "app is slow," so this matters for retention.

## Scope

We should look at improving performance across the board. Front-end, back-end, database — wherever there are wins to be had. The engineering team knows where the pain points are, so use your judgment on where to focus first. Prioritize the areas that will have the biggest impact on how fast the app feels to end users.

## Expectations

By the end of the quarter, the app should be noticeably faster. We want users to feel the difference. Please measure things appropriately and report back on the improvements.

## Success

Success looks like happier users and fewer complaints about speed. Let's make it fast.
